A letter from your Jaan

Happy Boyfriend's Day, my love. ♡

I don't always know how to say everything I feel, especially when you're right in front of me. Sometimes the words get stuck somewhere between my heart and my mouth. So today, I want to put it all here — where you can read it slowly and know exactly what I mean.

I love you so, so much. Like, lottt. Seriously. And I am so grateful that I get to have you in my life.

Thank you for being there for me, for taking care of me, for supporting me, and for loving me through all my little moods and all my little versions of myself. Thank you for being the person I can lean on and the person who makes me feel safe enough to just be me.

Thank you for being my peace, my chaos, my happiness, my biggest comfort — and somehow also the person who can make the simplest, tiniest moments feel so special and beautiful.

Today is the perfect excuse to remind you of something I hope you never forget: you mean so much to me. More than I probably manage to show you. More than I manage to say out loud.

And whenever life gets loud or difficult, I hope you remember that there is always one person cheering the loudest for you. There is always one person you can come back to without thinking twice. One person who believes you will make it happen, even when things feel hard.

That person is me. Your Jaan. Your Cutie. Your Panda. ♡

I will always be ridiculously proud of you. I will always want to see you happy. I will always be in your corner. I hope you know that.

Thank you for these almost three years of memories, laughter, comfort, nonsense, love, and all the little moments that somehow became my favourite parts of life.

If I don't say it enough in person,
let this page say it for me:
I love you with my everything. ♡

— your Jaan, Oshina ♡
